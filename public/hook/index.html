§ 6How the forecast works
  1. Who's in. Accounts × share eligible × share who request shares = clients putting in an indication of interest (IOI).
  2. Who calls. Some of those clients call, a few times each. Clients who never request still call to ask if they can, how to fund, or what's going on.
  3. Short fills. The firm's shares are deal size × retail slice × the firm's cut. If clients ask for more than that, the shortfall turns into extra "why did I only get 3 shares" calls on debut day and the day after.
  4. When. The calls are spread across the deal calendar: buzz, IOI window, IOI deadline, pricing night (when clients must confirm), debut, then a fade. Weekends and Thanksgiving are skipped.
  5. Staffing. Busiest-hour calls × handle time give the load, and Erlang C turns that into reps for an 80/30 service level.

Defaults are round placeholder numbers for a mid-to-large retail broker, not any real firm's figures. Put in your own.
