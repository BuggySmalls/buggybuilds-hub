How it works

  • Everyone starts on the same Wikipedia page. Get to the target page using only the links in the article.
  • No search, no back button, no typing addresses. Ctrl+F on the page is fair game.
  • Fastest time wins. Fewer clicks breaks a tie.
  • Routes stay hidden until you’ve made your own run. Finish or give up, then you get every path, page by page, with how long each person sat on each page.
  • The daily race is one go per name per day. Solo races (random, past dailies or your own pages) are as many as you want and stay on your device.
  • The shortest route stays hidden until you hit the button.
